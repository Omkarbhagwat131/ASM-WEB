A LEGACY OF LEARNING

Rooted in experience.
Ready for tomorrow.

ASM's NextGen Technical Campus is part of Audyogik Shikshan Mandal (ASM), an education group established in 1983. The campus is located in Talegaon Dabhade, Maval, Pune.

ASM's wider learning community spans education from KG to Ph.D. This campus brings together technology, management and student development.

Use this site to explore the project’s programs and modules. For current facts, course availability, eligibility and admissions, contact the campus directly.
