Eligibility Criteria

Engineering Qualification

Successfully completed higher secondary education (12th/HSC) or diploma with required marks and subjects as per admission rules.

Engineering Entrance

Fulfill applicable entrance examination requirements (MH-CET / JEE) and merit guidelines prescribed for admission.

MCA Graduation

Completed recognized Bachelor's degree (BCA/B.Sc/B.Com/B.A) with Mathematics/Statistics at 10+2 level or Graduation level.

MCA Entrance Requirements

Must qualify the applicable entrance examination as per university and government admission guidelines.
