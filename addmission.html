Engineering Admission

Engineering Eligibility

Academic Qualification

Candidates seeking admission to an eligible Engineering programme should have successfully completed the required higher secondary education or equivalent qualification.

Entrance / Admission Requirements

Candidates must fulfil the applicable entrance examination, merit and admission requirements prescribed for the selected Engineering programme.

Subject Requirements

Candidates should have the required subjects and academic background as specified for the Engineering programme.

MCA Admission

MCA Eligibility

Graduation

Candidates seeking admission to MCA should have completed the required bachelor's degree or equivalent qualification from a recognized institution.

Academic Requirements

Applicants must satisfy the academic requirements and applicable admission criteria for the MCA programme.

Mathematics / Subject Requirement

Applicable mathematics or subject requirements may vary according to the admission rules and programme criteria.
