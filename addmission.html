Admission Process

  1. Choose your preferred course.
  2. Check the eligibility criteria for that course.
  3. Contact the college admission office for current procedures.
  4. Submit the application and documents as instructed.
  5. Complete the official admission formalities.

Documents to Keep Ready

  • Previous qualifying examination marksheets
  • Leaving certificate, if applicable
  • Identity proof
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Other certificates, if required for your course

Note: Required documents depend on the course and current admission rules. Confirm the final list with the college.
