Course Details

About BCA

The Bachelor of Computer Applications introduces students to computing and the development of software and digital applications.

Typical duration: 3 years, subject to current university regulations.

Areas of study

  • Programming and data structures
  • Web development and databases
  • Software engineering and computer networks
  • Emerging technologies and project work

Possible career paths

Application or web developer, software tester, IT support specialist, or further study in computer applications.

About BBA

The Bachelor of Business Administration builds a foundation in how organizations operate, make decisions and grow.

Typical duration: 3 years, subject to current university regulations.

Areas of study

  • Management and business communication
  • Marketing, finance and human resources
  • Operations and business strategy
  • Entrepreneurship, case studies and projects

Possible career paths

Business development associate, marketing coordinator, operations assistant, or further study in management.

About B.Com

The Bachelor of Commerce develops knowledge of accounting, financial systems and the business environment.

Typical duration: 3 years, subject to current university regulations.

Areas of study

  • Financial and corporate accounting
  • Finance, economics and business law
  • Taxation and auditing fundamentals
  • Business communication and analysis

Possible career paths

Accounts assistant, banking or finance associate, tax support professional, or further study in commerce and finance.

About B.Sc Information Technology

This undergraduate programme introduces core information technology concepts and the systems used to build and support digital services.

Typical duration: 3 years, subject to current university regulations.

Areas of study

  • Programming and software development
  • Computer networks and operating systems
  • Databases and web technologies
  • Cloud, cybersecurity and practical projects

Possible career paths

IT support or network technician, junior software developer, systems assistant, or further study in information technology.

About MBA

The Master of Business Administration focuses on management practice, organizational decision-making and business leadership.

Typical duration: 2 years, subject to current university regulations.

Areas of study

  • Finance and marketing
  • Human resources and operations
  • Strategy, analytics and leadership
  • Case studies, presentations and projects

Possible career paths

Business or operations analyst, marketing or HR professional, project coordinator, or management roles aligned with prior experience.

About MCA

The Master of Computer Applications is a postgraduate programme focused on computer applications, programming and software development.

Typical duration: 2 years, subject to current university regulations.

Areas of study

  • Python and Java programming
  • Database management and web development
  • Software engineering and cloud computing
  • Artificial intelligence, data science and project work

Possible career paths

Software or web developer, cloud engineer, data analyst, systems analyst, or other roles in application development.
